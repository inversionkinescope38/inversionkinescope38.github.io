🤔 What Is T58?

T58 is a complete research environment for anyone interested in systematic trading. Whether you're exploring automated strategies, validating a new idea, or preparing for prop firm challenges, T58 helps you answer the most important question: "Will this strategy actually work?"

Unlike cloud-based tools, T58 runs entirely on your computer. This means your data, your strategies, and your results stay private. No uploads. No subscriptions. Just pure, fast, offline analysis.

🛠️ Powerful Features Built For Serious Traders

📊 Advanced Backtesting

Simulate your trading strategy on historical market data. See exactly how your rules would have performed, trade by trade, day by day.

🎲 Monte Carlo Simulation

Run thousands of alternative market scenarios to test the robustness of your strategy. Understand the range of possible outcomes and the risk of ruin.

🔒 Prop-Firm Constraint Testing

If you're aiming for prop firm funding, T58 helps you test your strategy against the strict rules of prop firms (daily loss limits, drawdowns, minimum trading days). Know your limits before you trade.

📅 Walk-Forward Validation

Prevent overfitting. T58 splits your market data into segments, optimizing your strategy on one period and testing it on the next, ensuring your strategy is genuinely predictive, not just reactive.
